# Kestrel harbour report

Summary: The Kestrel harbour authority dredged the main channel to
a depth of 14 metres in 2024.

**Traffic**

The Kestrel harbour handled 320 cargo ships and 45 ferries in 2025.
Its new crane lifted 900 containers in one day.

"The tide was the highest in decades," says Mara Velt, the harbour master.
Its height reached 6 metres.

The harbour could double its capacity by 2030.

Run the export tool with this command:

```sh
harbour export --year 2025
```

The `harbour.export(year=...)` call writes one row per ship to `report.csv`.
