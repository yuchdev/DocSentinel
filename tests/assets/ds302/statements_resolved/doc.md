# Harbour crane report

Summary: A team led by Mara Velt at the Kestrel harbour authority has
built a new tidal crane for the east quay. The innovation lifts 900
containers per day and can run on tidal power alone. Their findings were
published in Port Engineering.

The researchers tested the design on the east quay in 2025. To address
this limitation, the engineers added a second motor. In addition, the
crane weighs 40 tonnes. As a result, the quay needed new rails. It also
serves the ferry berth.

"The crane changed our schedule," says Jon Ferris, the harbour master.
He manages 12 berths.

The authority publishes tide tables every spring. Those tables list 4
daily tides. The authority could double the quay by 2030.

## Notes

It handles 45 ferries a year.
